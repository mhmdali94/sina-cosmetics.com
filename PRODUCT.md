# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

A mix of end consumers and wholesale/retail resellers browsing the Sina Cosmetics (شركة سيناء لمستحضرات التجميل) catalog. Arabic-speaking, RTL, largely on mobile. Consumers shop for everyday makeup, skincare, hair, and nail-care products; resellers use the same catalog to scan and source inventory. The context is browsing product categories and listings, not operating an account dashboard or app-like tool.

## Product Purpose

A catalog-first storefront for an Egyptian cosmetics manufacturer. Its job is to build brand awareness and trust rather than close transactions on-site — success looks like a visitor coming away confident the brand is a credible, established manufacturer, whether or not a purchase happens through the site itself.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy and affordable. Dependable, straightforward, accessible pricing — positioned as a reliable local manufacturer rather than an aspirational luxury import. Warm but not fussy; confident without being flashy.

## Anti-references

No specific reference sites given. Default guardrail: avoid the cluttered, low-trust AliExpress-style marketplace look (stock placeholder images, cramped repetitive grids, banner-ad clutter). The trustworthy/affordable personality still needs to read as credible, not cheap.

## Design Principles

- Credibility over flash: every design choice should reinforce "established, real manufacturer," not "generic internet storefront."
- Affordable, not cheap: clean pricing and product presentation without slipping into discount-bin visual clutter.
- Catalog clarity first: finding and browsing products by category, in Arabic/RTL, is the primary job; conversion pressure is secondary to trust-building.
- Serve both audiences: legible to a one-off retail shopper and fast to scan for a reseller checking inventory.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard good practice: solid color contrast, readable Arabic typography at body sizes, comfortably sized touch targets on mobile. No formal WCAG level was requested. RTL correctness is a first-class concern given the site is Arabic-only.
